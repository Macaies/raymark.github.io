01Company Profile

Solid Video Corporation (SVC)

Solid Video Corporation (SVC) is a Philippine-based broadcast and AV systems integrator delivering professional video infrastructure, control room systems, mobile broadcast units, and institutional AV deployments.

SVC provides turnkey systems including Outside Broadcast (OB) Vans, Electronic News Gathering (ENG) Vans, Master Control Room (MCR) integration, studio configuration, digital intercom systems, signal distribution, and operational workflows.

The company supports government institutions, national broadcasters, enterprise clients, and major live events, with a strong focus on system reliability, integration precision, documentation, and operational continuity.
